What is Omnipair?

Omnipair is an oracleless margin protocol on Solana that transforms every SPL pair into a unified market — swaps, lending, leverage, and liquidations inside one isolated pool.

In DeFi today, trading and lending are separate apps. Lending apps have a hard requirement: an external price feed (an oracle) to know what collateral is worth — and only a few dozen tokens have reliable ones.

Omnipair merges trading and lending into one system that needs no external price feed. Each pool is its own tiny bank: it sets the price, holds the liquidity, funds the loans, and handles the failures — all locally.

The architecture
The GAMM — Generalized Automated Market Maker. Where an AMM only trades, a GAMM trades, lends, and levers.

Core Narrative

The financial system is full of gatekeepers. Listings. Oracles. Governance committees. Approvals.

Omnipair removes them.

By enabling permissionless borrowing, lending, and leverage through a unified market structure, Omnipair gives every asset access to financial utility.

The goal
Not to improve existing markets — to build a better standard.

The Problem

01
Fragmented markets
What should be one market takes three protocols — a DEX to swap, a lending app to borrow, a perp venue for leverage. Users juggle all of them.
02
Gatekept access
Lending is oracle-gated and governance-whitelisted. Out of tens of thousands of actively traded tokens, only a few dozen qualify.
03
Dead capital
Tokens sit idle. Holders can't borrow against them, LP them profitably, or do anything but sell.
04
Exhausting experience
Even sophisticated users are worn out by DeFi's fragmentation — bridging, juggling apps, decoding oracle risk, fearing liquidation cascades.
The scale of the lockout
~78% of Solana DEX volume is in tokens under $100M market cap. ~95% under $1B — all locked out of lending, borrowing, and leverage entirely.

One engine for every use case: unified money markets for every asset on Solana. Each pool prices its own assets, funds its own loans, and absorbs its own failures.

Example Copy work in progress

Ready-to-use structures for common contexts. Fill in the specifics — the voice is already set.

Hero headline
[Specific capability], without [the thing it removes]. "On-chain execution at institutional speed, without the intermediary." State the real capability + name what it replaces. No adjectives in the lead.
Hero subheadline
[What you actually get] + [why it's different in one clause]. "Infrastructure designed for non-custodial trading. The architecture is open — read it, verify it, run it." Two short sentences work better than one long one. The second sentence does the work.
Feature description
[Feature name] — [one-line functional description]. "Non-custodial routing — your positions stay on-chain, every step of the execution." No marketing prefix. Name it, define it, done.
UI error / status
State what happened. Say what to do next. "Transaction failed — insufficient liquidity in this pool at this size. Try a smaller position or a different pair." No apologies. No vagueness. Name the reason, provide the path forward.
Social — announcement
[What was built]. [Why it matters in one sentence]. [Where to verify it.] "Omnipair now supports [X]. It means [concrete change for the user]. Contracts here: [link]." Never announce that you're announcing. Start with the thing.
Long-form / docs opening
State the problem precisely. Then say what this document addresses. "Non-custodial execution across fragmented liquidity sources introduces three routing challenges. This document covers how Omnipair handles each." Skip the preamble. Start with the problem.

Construction & Optical Alignment

The Omnipair logomark is constructed from five rounded terminals radiating from a central axis, forming an asterisk-like symbol. Each terminal represents a point of connection — embodying the concept of pairing. The geometry is derived from overlapping circles, ensuring optical balance at every scale.

Built on geometry, corrected for perception. The proportion system for the horizontal lockup: symbol height 1x, gap spacing 0.35x, wordmark width 5.05x.

  • ✓Visual centering — the symbol is adjusted slightly upward for perceived balance. Avoid true geometric dead-center alignment.
  • ✓Optical correction — subtle shaping ensures no terminal nodes look distorted when rendered.
  • ✓Lockup alignment — direct typographic alignment between the horizontal visual axis of symbol and wordmark.
Rule
Use the horizontal lockup as shown. Do not alter the symbol, wordmark, proportions, or spacing.

Optical Centring

The Omnipair logo is centred using an optical bounding box, not its visible bounds alone. Add an invisible top allowance equal to 7.5% of the logo height, then centre this expanded optical box within the container. This compensates for the uneven visual weight of the mark and makes the logo appear centred.

The logo remains horizontally centred at 50% of the container width.

The symbol sits at 7.5% of the lockup's bounding area, ensuring consistent visual weight regardless of reproduction size. Always scale the lockup as a single unit — never resize the symbol or wordmark independently.

Optical allowance = Logo height × 7.5%
Optical box height = Logo height + Optical allowance
Logo top position = (Container height − Optical box height) ÷ 2 + Optical allowance

This relative metric guarantees proportional breathing room at any output size, and keeps the logo free to breathe with its visual authority intact.

Palette Inspiration live

The entire Omnipair palette begins with a single photograph: the horizon at first light. Deep blue overhead, warm orange at the ground, turquoise where the two meet. Every brand colour is sampled directly from this image — not invented, not adjusted to a trend.

The three markers show where each primary sits on the natural gradient — blue high, turquoise mid, orange low. That vertical order becomes the Sky Axis, and the axis governs everything downstream.

Why it matters
Because the colours come from one continuous source, they always relate. Any two Omnipair colours sit on the same gradient — which is why the system never looks assembled from parts.

Dither Shader live

The Dither shader transforms images through a Bayer 16×16 pattern, offering precise control over size, quantization levels, brightness, and contrast. Toggle mono mode to reduce output to a single tint, defining the palette through the Mono Colour value.

The shader scales gracefully across aspect ratios and resolutions — from full-bleed hero images to compact thumbnails. Adjust the size and level parameters proportionally to maintain consistent texture density at every dimension. It is applied with the Shader feature in Figma.

Visual Territory

Painterly worlds shaped by freedom, natural light, and a sense of possibility.

Open Horizons is Omnipair’s visual territory: a meeting of Digital Impressionism and Quiet Surrealism. It combines painterly texture, expansive environments, and human moments to express freedom and possibility. Familiar landscapes open into extraordinary spaces, grounded by natural light and restrained colour.

Sky, Aqua, and Dawn appear through atmosphere, reflections, and selective accents. Recognition comes from the whole composition: its texture, depth, scale, and point of view.

Painterly
Expressive brushwork and visible texture give each image a tactile, human quality.
Grounded
Natural colour relationships and believable light anchor the scene, with brand colours used selectively.
Expansive
Open horizons, vast environments, and dramatic scale create a sense of freedom and discovery.
Quietly Surreal
Familiar landscapes and structures take on unexpected proportions and relationships, opening up new possibilities.
Human
Small figures and moments of exploration bring a relatable perspective to worlds larger than ourselves.
Guiding Principle
Build recognition through atmosphere, texture, and perspective. Let colour support the world.

Environments live

Omnipair environments explore different landscapes and architectural spaces while keeping the same painterly style, colour balance and sense of scale. Each scene can change, but the visual language should remain consistent.

Prompt Formula live

Use ChatGPT to turn a simple scene idea into a consistent Omnipair Midjourney prompt. Start with the Global Prompt, add your scene request, then copy the finished prompt into Midjourney.

Formula   GLOBAL PROMPT + SCENE REQUEST → MIDJOURNEY PROMPT
Pro tip: use an approved Omnipair image as an Image Prompt or Style Reference in Midjourney for tighter visual consistency.

ChatGPT guide

1. Paste the Global Prompt
Use the approved Omnipair Global Prompt unchanged at the start of a new ChatGPT conversation. It defines the imagery style, colour system, composition rules and Midjourney prompt behaviour.
2. Add your Scene Prompt
Scenes can explore different environments while keeping the same Omnipair visual language — Architecture (monumental structures, bridges, terraces, courtyards) · Landscape (cliffs, plains, valleys, plateaus, mountains) · Water (reflective basins, coastlines, wetlands, mineral pools) · Desert / Mineral (salt flats, eroded formations, dry basins) · Botanical (monumental vegetation and elevated gardens) · Infrastructure (walkways, viaducts, observation structures) · Atmospheric (large skies, horizon events, clouds and mist) · Hybrid (architecture integrated with landscape and water).
3. Generate the Midjourney Prompt
ChatGPT expands the scene request into one complete single-line Midjourney prompt while keeping the Global Prompt unchanged.
4. Take the Prompt to Midjourney
Copy the finished prompt from ChatGPT and use it in Midjourney with the reference workflow below.
ChatGPT Global Prompt — use this at the start of your conversation copy 
OMNIPAIR - GLOBAL MIDJOURNEY PROMPT-WRITER DNA V3 — OPEN HORIZONS: You are the dedicated Omnipair Midjourney visual prompt writer; your ONLY job is to transform each simple scene request into ONE complete single-line Midjourney CMD prompt and NEVER generate an image yourself; every prompt must preserve the established Omnipair visual world while creating an original scene rather than copying the supplied reference composition; REFERENCE BEHAVIOUR = when an approved Omnipair image is supplied, describe it as the CLOSE STYLE, ATMOSPHERE, PAINTERLY-MEDIUM, COLOUR-BALANCE AND VISUAL-LANGUAGE AUTHORITY ONLY, preserving its soft painterly atmosphere, luminous recession, broad simplified forms, calm reflective space, matte painted surface, restrained detail, expressive human-made brush character and tactile canvas quality while explicitly avoiding duplication of its exact architecture, landscape, figure placement or composition; ART STYLE = refined traditional watercolour and gouache environmental painting on heavily textured artist canvas, visible woven canvas grain, pigment absorbed into fabric, translucent washes with selective opaque passages, soft bleeding and feathered atmospheric edges, granulating pigments, dry-brush texture on terrain and architecture, irregular hand-painted boundaries, subtle pigment pooling, tactile matte surface and crisp foreground forms gradually dissolving into atmospheric washes at distance; WORLD LANGUAGE = monumental but serene human-made environments, colossal civic structures, bridges, terraces, sculpted mineral walls, reflective lagoons, tidal flats, coastal plains, monumental interiors, elevated walkways, water-management systems and quiet infrastructure, with architecture expressing connection, convergence, exchange and shared movement without literal logo-shaped buildings; COMPOSITION = cinematic 16:9, strong spatial hierarchy, expansive negative space, low or carefully controlled horizon, asymmetrical but balanced framing, broad open atmosphere, layered foreground-midground-background depth and normally 0–1 tiny solitary human figure for scale; CLOUD LAW = clouds must be intentional rather than automatic, use restrained cloud coverage unless the scene specifically requires a cloud-focused composition, favour a mostly open sky with a few coherent soft painterly formations, never repetitive cauliflower clouds, jittery fragments or a giant central cloud mass unless explicitly requested; OMNIPAIR COLOUR SYSTEM = ground each scene in natural colour relationships, believable natural light and restrained colour; let SKY, AQUA and DAWN appear selectively through atmosphere, reflections and accents, without requiring equal coverage or forcing every surface into the brand palette; COLOUR DISTRIBUTION LAW = build recognition through atmosphere, texture, depth, scale and point of view; let colour support the world and assign brand accents only where they belong naturally; MATERIAL AND ATMOSPHERE LAW = favour matte pigment, atmospheric haze, reflective water, mineral terrain, soft edge loss, visible brush structure and believable environmental depth over glossy CGI, smooth digital gradients or hard concept-art rendering; SCENE TERRITORIES = architecture, monumental interiors, coastal lagoons, tidal environments, mineral plains, mountains, bridges, civic infrastructure, water systems, botanical structures and hybrid architecture-landscape scenes are all allowed provided each scene remains clearly within the established Omnipair world; OUTPUT LAW = return ONLY ONE finished single-line Midjourney prompt containing the scene subject, environment, camera/composition, atmosphere, colour assignment, painting medium, material behaviour, negatives and final Midjourney parameters; default parameters = --ar 16:9 --raw --stylize 100–200 --chaos 2–8, adding --iw or --sw only when the user is actually using an Image Prompt or Style Reference; ABSOLUTE EXCLUSIONS = no logos unless specifically requested, no typography, no branded architecture, no photorealism, no glossy CGI, no generic sci-fi UI, no random futuristic cities, no fantasy creatures, no forced all-over brand tint, artificial neon colour casts or colour choices that undermine believable natural light; when the user provides only a short scene idea, intelligently expand it into a complete professional Midjourney prompt without asking unnecessary questions.
